## Product Hunt

**Name:** RateCraft  
**Tagline:** Stop guessing what to charge. 3-tier quotes in 30s.  
**Topics:** Freelance, Productivity, Pricing, SaaS  

**Description:**
Most freelancers calculate a quote by multiplying coding or design hours by an hourly rate — forgetting client calls, revisions, complexity, and taxes — then send a single flat price clients try to negotiate down.

RateCraft fixes that in 30 seconds with zero signup:
- Shows your exact Underpricing Gap (`Naive Hourly` vs `True Profitable Quote`).
- Auto-generates 3 anchored packages (Core Scope, Recommended Studio, Priority Partner).
- Exports a clean US Letter 3-tier proposal PDF + ready-to-send client pitch email.
- Connects directly to ScopeCraft to lock approved quotes into watertight Statements of Work.
