# RateCraft — Freelance Pricing & 3-Tier Quote Studio

**Live Production App:** [https://ratecraft-app.vercel.app](https://ratecraft-app.vercel.app)

Built by **CraftApp Studio**, RateCraft helps freelancers, consultants, and boutique agencies stop underpricing by calculating hidden overhead, revision buffers, complexity, and profit margins into client-ready Basic, Standard, and Priority quote packages.

## Key Capabilities

- **Hidden Underpricing Gap Calculator:** Compares naive `hours × rate` against true delivery economics so freelancers see exact revenue left on the table.
- **3-Tier Proposal PDF Export:** Client-side US Letter PDF (`jspdf`) presenting Core Scope, Recommended Studio, and Priority Partner packages side-by-side.
- **Ready-to-Send Pitch Email & Viral Share Summary:** One-click client pitch email anchoring the Recommended tier plus shareable quote summary.
- **30 Industry Pricing Playbooks (`/rates/[slug]`):** Static SSG guides with US market ranges, underpricing traps, pre-built package tiers, JSON-LD Schema, XML Sitemap, and OpenGraph cards.
- **Connected to ScopeCraft:** One-click handoff (`Generate SOW in ScopeCraft`) passes the chosen project type straight into [ScopeCraft](https://scopecraft-sow.vercel.app) to lock down exclusions and deposit milestones.
